📚 Clinderma Clinical Protocol Guidelines

Acne Vulgaris Protocol (Comedonal & Inflammatory)
  1. AM Routine: Clinderma Gentle Gel Cleanser → 10% Niacinamide + Zinc Serum → Oil-Free Hydrator → Broad-Spectrum SPF 50.
  2. PM Routine: Gentle Gel Cleanser → 2% Salicylic Acid (BHA) Serum (alternate nights) → Ceramide Barrier Cream.
  3. Caution: Avoid combining salicylic acid with prescription oral isotretinoin.
Post-Inflammatory Hyperpigmentation (PIH) & Melasma
  1. AM Routine: Hydrating Cleanser → 2% Alpha Arbutin Serum → Tinted Mineral SPF 50+ (Blocks visible blue light).
  2. PM Routine: Gentle Cleanser → 3% Tranexamic Acid Serum → 10% Azelaic Acid Cream → Barrier Moisturizer.
  3. Clinical Note: Reapply sunscreen every 3 hours when outdoors.
Compromised Skin Barrier / Retinoid Dermatitis
  1. Immediate Action: Discontinue all AHA/BHA exfoliants, vitamin C, and retinoids for 7-10 days.
  2. AM/PM Protocol: Lukewarm water rinse or non-foaming cleanser → 5x Ceramide + Cica (Centella) Barrier Balm.
  3. Occlusive Therapy: Thin layer of pure white petroleum jelly on dry micro-cracks overnight.
