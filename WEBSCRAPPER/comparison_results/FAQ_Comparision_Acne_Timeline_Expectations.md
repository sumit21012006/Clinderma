### 2. [faq_04] What is purging?
- **Category**: `Acne - Timeline & Expectations` | **Cohort**: `General`
- **Alignment Score**: `85.0%`

#### 📖 Ground-Truth Master Answer:
> Purging is a temporary increase in acne when treatment starts.
> 
> Certain active ingredients like oral/topical retinoids, AHAs, BHAs increase skin cell turnover, bringing hidden clogged pores to the surface faster.
> 
> Purging usually happens in the first 2-4 weeks, is temporary, and settles with continued treatment.

#### 🤖 Live Chatbot Response:
Purging is a temporary increase in breakouts that can happen after starting active acne treatments like retinoids or exfoliating acids. These ingredients speed up skin renewal, so clogged pores surface faster before the skin clears.

Usually:
- Starts within 2–4 weeks
- Improves within 3–6 weeks
- Happens in areas where acne already exists

If breakouts become very painful, swollen, or continue worsening beyond a few weeks, the treatment should be reviewed by a dermatologist.
