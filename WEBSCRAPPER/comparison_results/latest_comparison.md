- **Alignment Score**: `100.0%`

#### 📖 Ground-Truth Master Answer:
> Purging is a temporary increase in acne when treatment starts.
>

> 
> Purging usually happens in the first 2-4 weeks, is temporary, and settles with continued treatment.

#### 🤖 Live Chatbot Response:
Purging is a temporary increase in pimples that can happen when acne treatment starts, especially with ingredients like retinoids, AHAs, or BHAs. These ingredients speed up skin turnover and bring hidden clogged pores to the surface faster.

Usually:
- Starts within 2–4 weeks of treatment
- Settles gradually in about 3–6 weeks
- Improves with continued treatment

Purging is different from a bad reaction. If breakouts become very painful, spread to new areas, or continue worsening, a dermatologist should review the treatment.
